Cortana eBay Connector Privacy Notice

Effective October 20, 2026.

This notice describes a private, single-owner assistant connector for managing the owner's eBay listings. This public page contains no account records or credentials and does not provide access to the connector.

Information and purpose

When authorized, the connector processes eBay account identity, listing and image information, app credentials, and authorization tokens to prepare, validate, and carry out the owner's requested listing work. Identity is checked before account access is activated. Publishing a listing requires the owner's specific instruction.

Storage and service providers

Client secrets and refresh tokens are encrypted server-side in the connector's database. The separate encryption key is held in server-only secret storage. Short-lived access tokens are used server-side. OpenAI Sites and its Cloudflare hosting infrastructure process data to operate the connector; eBay receives authorized API requests. Credentials are not intentionally placed in assistant responses, browser bundles, or application logs. Hosting providers may maintain operational logs or backups under their own policies.

Access and retention

The connector is owner-private. OAuth state expires after ten minutes and is consumed once. Encrypted connection records remain until removed by the owner or the connector is retired. Account authorization can also be revoked through eBay. Revocation prevents renewed API access but does not itself erase records already stored.

Choices and questions

The owner can request disconnection, deletion, or correction through the assistant used to operate this connector. The connector is not offered as a service to other eBay users. No advertising, analytics, or tracking scripts are included on this notice page. Infrastructure may process routine request metadata to serve the page securely.
